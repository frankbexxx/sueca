Privacy Policy — Suecão

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Terms of Use Contact

This Privacy Policy describes how Suecão handles information in the current Play Store v1 release (guest / local-first). If a future release adds accounts, cloud sync, multiplayer, advertising, analytics or remote media, this policy will be updated before those features are publicly offered.

1. Who operates Suecão

Suecão is operated by Francisco Bexiga. OXS — Oeiras Xtreme Software is the project brand used for Suecão. It is not described here as a registered company or separate legal entity.

Privacy questions: frankbex.dev@gmail.com

2. Summary (Play v1)

  • Gameplay, history and preferences are stored on your device.
  • Suecão’s own Play v1 app features do not send personal information to Suecão-operated servers.
  • There is no public Suecão account, cloud sync, multiplayer lobby, advertising or developer analytics in this release.
  • Android / Google Play may process installation and device information under their own policies; that is separate from Suecão’s in-app data flows.

3. Information processed on your device

Suecão stores information locally so the app can work offline and remember your progress. Categories include:

  • Match history and statistics
  • Saved or resumable games (where offered)
  • Preferences such as language, theme, card face and card back, audio and music settings
  • A local guest identifier used only on the device
  • Optional on-device diagnostic information (not sent to Suecão as remote telemetry in Play v1)

Purpose: run the games, remember settings, show history/statistics, and support local diagnostics.

4. Information sent by Suecão app features (Play v1)

Through Suecão’s shipping Play v1 application features, personal information is not collected onto Suecão-operated servers. There is no Suecão account login, no cloud backup of history, and no Suecão analytics or crash-reporting SDK in this release.

This does not mean that “nothing ever leaves the device” in every technical sense:

  • Google Play / Android may process install, update, device or usage-related metadata under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • The app declares the usual INTERNET permission as part of the WebView / network stack; Play v1 does not use that permission to upload your game history or preferences to Suecão.

5. Accounts

Play v1 does not offer a public Suecão account. Suecão does not collect Google account identity, email or display name through Play v1 features.

Future versions may introduce optional account or sync features. If so, this policy will be updated before those features ship publicly.

6. Multiplayer and online play

Multiplayer / online rooms are not enabled in Play v1.

7. Analytics, advertising and identifiers

In Suecão Play v1:

  • No developer-integrated analytics SDK
  • No crash-reporting SDK
  • No in-app advertising
  • No use of advertising identifiers by Suecão

Google Play or the operating system may still perform their own measurement under Google’s policies.

8. Music and remote content

Play v1 uses bundled / on-device music and sound. Suecão does not use a remote music CDN for this frozen configuration.

9. Permissions (Android)

Suecão Play v1 uses:

  • INTERNET — standard networking / WebView capability (not used in Play v1 to upload your local game data to Suecão)

Suecão Play v1 does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or broad media/storage access.

10. Retention and deletion

Local data generally remains until you clear the app’s data in system settings or uninstall the app.

Some in-app reset actions (if offered) may remove part of that data. Not every preference is necessarily cleared by every internal reset. Clearing app data or uninstalling is the reliable way to remove on-device Suecão data in Play v1.

Because there is no public Suecão account in Play v1, there is no separate “delete my cloud account” flow for this release.

11. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used in the application. Local data is primarily protected by your device and platform security. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and no special security certification is claimed.

12. Children

Suecão is not specifically directed to children. Play v1 does not include an age gate. Parents and guardians remain responsible for complying with any age or consent rules that apply to them.

13. Third-party and platform services

Distribution and device services (for example Google Play and Android) are governed by the providers’ own terms and privacy policies. Suecão does not control how those platforms process information.

14. Your rights (EU / GDPR context)

Depending on applicable law (including the GDPR where it applies), you may have rights such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection or complaint to a supervisory authority.

For Play v1, Suecão does not hold remote personal-data profiles from the app’s own features. On-device information is typically removed by clearing app data or uninstalling.
